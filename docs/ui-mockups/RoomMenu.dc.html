The place's one action

One Room menu, one order, both devices

The desktop bar used to carry seven controls on its right-hand side, with Leave at the end of a row of routine ones. Four of them moved in here. The invite comes first because it is what a waiting room is for; Leave is last, set apart, and still routed through the confirmation dialog.

On a wide screen

A dropdown under the Room button — the one action, with Player settings and the identity chip beside it, never moving.

On a phone

The same rows in a sheet under the thumb, opened by the bar's single ⋯ button. It carries two more, because the phone bar has nowhere else to put them: Players and scores and Player settings.
